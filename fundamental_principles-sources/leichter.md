# Otto Leichter

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Otto Leichter** (22 February 1897, Vienna – 14 February 1973, New York) was an Austrian socialist, journalist and author who worked for the Social Democratic Workers' Party (SDAP) and its *Arbeiter-Zeitung*. He went into exile after 1934 and 1938, and from 1957 to 1971 was UN correspondent for the Deutsche Presse-Agentur. His first wife was the social scientist Käthe Leichter, murdered by the Nazis in 1942. [Wikipedia (German)](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Leichter); no English article.

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The most-quoted author after Marx. The GIC praises him as “the first fruit of Weber's criticism”: he bases production on working-time accounting and describes cost accounting between plants. But he keeps wage differences and central price policy, so the GIC concludes his scheme leads to state capitalism.
