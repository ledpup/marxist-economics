# Heinrich Cunow

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Heinrich Cunow** (11 April 1862, Schwerin – 20 August 1936) was an SPD politician and prominent Marxist theorist. Having first opposed the First World War, he came to argue that German imperialism was progressive, and edited the theoretical journal *Die Neue Zeit*. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Cunow)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted, as a reformist, to show that Marx wanted production regulated by free associations, not the state.
