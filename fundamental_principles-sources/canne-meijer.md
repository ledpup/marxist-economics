# Henk Canne Meijer

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Henk Canne Meijer** (30 December 1890 – 28 December 1962) was a Dutch metalworker who left party communism after the Russian Revolution for the council communist opposition. He co-founded the GIC in September 1927 and edited the *Fundamental Principles* before publication. There is no Wikipedia article on him; see the [Group of International Communists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_of_International_Communists) article, the [libcom.org biography](https://libcom.org/article/henk-canne-meijer) and his writings at the [Marxists Internet Archive](https://www.marxists.org/archive/canne-meijer/index.htm).

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted by the editor, Hermann Lueer, in his 2020 foreword; not by the GIC itself.
