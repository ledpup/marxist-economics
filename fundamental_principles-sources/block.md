# Herbert Block

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**H. Block** is Herbert Block, whose *Die Marxsche Geldtheorie* (Jena: Gustav Fischer, 1926) began as a doctoral thesis at the University of Freiburg ([KIT library record](https://katalog.bibliothek.kit.edu/bib/479808)). No Wikipedia article or other biography was found.

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** “The economist H. Block” stands for the bourgeois case that without the market there can be no economic calculation. In ch. 4c the GIC spells him “Bloch”.
