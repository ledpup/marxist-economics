# Sébastien Faure

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Sébastien Faure** (6 January 1858, Loire – 14 July 1942, Royan) was a French anarcho-communist and freethought activist, a leading proponent of the “anarchist synthesis” for organising anarchist federations. Wikipedia's lead also notes that he was a convicted sex offender. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9bastien_Faure)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The GIC's example of libertarian communism. It argues that Faure's “free agreement” ends in a main administration that assigns production and consumption, the same as Hilferding's General Cartel.
