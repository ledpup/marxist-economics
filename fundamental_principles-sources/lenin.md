# Vladimir Lenin

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Vladimir Lenin** (born Vladimir Ilyich Ulyanov; 22 April 1870, Simbirsk – 21 January 1924, Gorki) was a Russian revolutionary, politician and Marxist theorist who founded and led the Soviet government, first over Soviet Russia and then the Soviet Union, until his death. His ideas formed the basis of Leninism. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Lenin)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The main example of state communism. The GIC credits *State and Revolution* for recovering Marx's lessons from the Paris Commune, but quotes Lenin to show the Bolsheviks aiming at state capitalism (“the postal service”) and admitting that the state machine ran them.
