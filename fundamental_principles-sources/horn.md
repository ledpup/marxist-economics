# Erich Horn

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Erich Horn** wrote *Die ökonomischen Grenzen der Gemeinwirtschaft* (Halberstadt, 1928). No Wikipedia article or other biography was found.

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted for calling communism, as Marxists had described it, “a negative system”: no money, no value, no market, no price.
