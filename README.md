# Marxist Economics

> "What we have to deal with here is a communist society as it *emerges* from capitalist society."
> — adapted from Karl Marx, [*Critique of the Gotha Programme*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1875/gotha/)

This repository collects texts on the economics of the first phase of communism: how a society that has just emerged from capitalism would organise production and distribution. At the centre is Marx's idea of labour certificates. Each producer gets back from society, after deductions for the common funds, as much as they gave it, measured in labour time.

## Documents

| Author | Title | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Karl Marx | [Critique of the Gotha Programme (extract on labour certificates)](Documents/Critique_of_the_Gotha_Programme-certificates.md) | An extract from Part I. Marx describes labour certificates and the "equal right" of the lower phase of communism, along with its limits. He contrasts this with the higher phase: "from each according to his ability, to each according to his needs". [Full text on marxists.org](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1875/gotha/) |
| Group of International Communists (GIC), ed. and trans. Hermann Lueer | [Fundamental Principles of Communist Production and Distribution](Documents/fundamental_principles.md) | The council communists' development of Marx's idea. It sets out the socially average working hour as the unit of account for production and consumption, the abolition of the market, and the management of production by the workers' councils themselves. It also critiques both state socialism and libertarian communism. [Read by chapter](Documents/fundamental_principles/README.md) |
