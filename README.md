# Marxist Economics

> "What we have to deal with here is a communist society as it *emerges* from capitalist society."
> — adapted from Karl Marx, [*Critique of the Gotha Programme*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1875/gotha/)

This repository collects texts on the economics of the first phase of communism: how a society that has just emerged from capitalism would organise production and distribution. At the centre is Marx's idea of labour certificates. Each producer gets back from society, after deductions for the common funds, as much as they gave it, measured in labour time.

### Comparisons and extensions

The *Fundamental Principles* set beside other systems and experiments, or carried into questions it doesn't address.

| Area | What it holds | Research date | Author |

| [Unemployment, dismissal and those unable to work](notes-opus5_5/unemployment-and-inability-to-work.md) | Whether the GIC deals with being dismissed, finding another operational unit, living without work, and being too ill, injured or old to work. It barely does. The note collects what the book does settle (no direction of labour, no closures "from above", free health care, a disaster fund) and what it leaves out, including Marx's "funds for those unable to work". It sets out how Pannekoek, Cockshott and Cottrell and today's labour-time circle fill the gap, shows the payout factor with non-workers included, and weighs the scheme against today's scale: ILO unemployment figures, Davis's *Planet of Slums* and Graeber's "bullshit jobs". | 2026-11-03 | Claude (Opus 5.5) |
