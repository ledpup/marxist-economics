# Market-clearing prices in the GIC's model

Cockshott and Cottrell's marketing algorithm can be added to the GIC's labour-time accounting without breaking it, provided three conditions hold:

1. The markup or discount is applied only when goods pass to consumers.
2. The labour value and the price paid are both recorded.
3. The net offset is settled only through the payout factor.

The GIC's real objection is not to the accounting. It is about who sets the price, and on what rule.

## The proposal

In *Towards a New Socialism*, every consumer good carries its labour value. It sells, however, at roughly the price in labour tokens that clears the market. The ratio of price to value tells the planners which goods to make more of (ratio above the average) and which to make less of (ratio below). Over time this pushes each price towards its value. ([TNS ch. 8](../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/08_The_Marketing_of_Consumer_Goods.md); [the algorithm in steps](../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/08_The_Marketing_of_Consumer_Goods.md#consumer-goods-and-the-macro-plan))

## Why the accounting still works

- **Enterprises are untouched.** Each operational unit is still credited with (P + C) + L. It never sees the market price, so it still has no "gain" or "loss" ([GIC ch. 11 d](../Documents/fundamental_principles/11_Accounting_as_Summary_of_Production_and_Distribution.md)). TNS also keeps the ratio out of enterprise income ([TNS ch. 8](../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/08_The_Marketing_of_Consumer_Goods.md#comparison-with-capitalist-markets)). Goods moving between enterprises stay at labour value.
- **The offsets form their own account.** Across all goods, markups minus discounts equal Token Consumption minus Consumption. Any balance left at the end of a period goes into the next period's [payout factor](../Documents/fundamental_principles/10_The_General_Social_Work.md#g-the-payout-factor). The payout factor already reconciles the hours worked with the product available for individual consumption.
- **The two signals complement each other.** The GIC links production to needs through the orders the cooperatives place, and through enterprises asking to expand at the next plan ([ch. 12 g](../Documents/fundamental_principles/12_The_Abolition_of_the_Market.md#g-the-cooperatives-and-the-benchmark-of-needs); [ch. 14 b](../Documents/fundamental_principles/14b_The_Control_of_Operational_Life_II.md#b-control-under-communism)). An order book shows that a good ran short, but not *how much* people want it. The price/value ratio shows that, and could back an expansion request within the GIC's limit ([ch. 13 c](../Documents/fundamental_principles/13_The_Expansion_of_Production.md#c-the-general-rule-for-the-expansion-of-the-operational-unit)).

## Where it conflicts

**Price policy.** The GIC insists the relation between producer and product be fixed "in the things themselves", so that "there is no room for price policy" ([ch. 9 e](../Documents/fundamental_principles/09_Average_Working_Hour_as_Basis_of_Consumption.md#e-the-wage-by-leichter)). They reject Varga's surcharge even though Varga applied the same percentage to every product ([ch. 10 c](../Documents/fundamental_principles/10_The_General_Social_Work.md#c-the-usual-solution)). They give two reasons:

- A surcharge hides how much labour each product takes. Marking both value and price answers this.
- Whoever sets prices controls distribution ([ch. 9 h](../Documents/fundamental_principles/09_Average_Working_Hour_as_Basis_of_Consumption.md#h-the-pricing-policy-in-hungary); [ch. 10 c](../Documents/fundamental_principles/10_The_General_Social_Work.md#c-the-usual-solution)). Recording both figures doesn't answer this. TNS's markups differ from good to good, and a central marketing authority sets them by trial and error. That is price policy in the GIC's sense.

**Hour for hour holds only on average.** With markups, one certificate hour buys less than an hour of social labour on popular goods and more on unpopular ones. The account nets to zero, but only as a transfer between consumers according to their tastes. TNS accepts this because incomes are equal ([TNS Table 8.1](../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/08_The_Marketing_of_Consumer_Goods.md#market-clearing-prices)). It still weakens the exact relation between producer and product that the GIC calls the demand of the revolution ([ch. 9 b](../Documents/fundamental_principles/09_Average_Working_Hour_as_Basis_of_Consumption.md#b-the-task-of-the-revolution)).

**Saving.** Prices that move give people a reason to hold on to certificates and wait for a discount. TNS even counts on this to steady prices. Unspent tokens throw out the balance between Token Consumption and Consumption. TNS limits this by having tokens expire after a set date: they "could have a 'use by' date" ([TNS ch. 2](../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/02_Eliminating_Inequalities.md)). Hoarding is "avoided by having the labour tokens expire after a specified date", and any saving goes through deposits the planners can monitor ([TNS ch. 7](../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/07_Macroeconomic_Planning_and_Budgetary_Policy.md#household-saving-and-credit)). With expiry, waiting for a discount is possible only within the validity period, and lapsed tokens become one more balance for the payout factor. Neither English edition of the *Fundamental Principles* in this collection mentions expiry. Adopting TNS's markups would mean adopting its expiry too.

## Conditions for a GIC-compatible version

1. Price both enterprises and transfers between them at labour value. Apply offsets only at the point of distribution to consumers.
2. Mark every good with its labour value and its price, and publish both.
3. Set prices by a published mechanical rule (for example, from stock levels), not by an authority's judgement. Let the cooperatives, not a central body, apply it.
4. Give certificates an expiry date, as TNS does, so that waiting for discounts can't pile up.
5. Never spend the net offset balance. Use it only to correct the next payout factor. Otherwise it becomes a hidden tax, the route of Leichter and Varga that the GIC rejects ([ch. 10 f](../Documents/fundamental_principles/10_The_General_Social_Work.md#f-the-marxist-solution); [ch. 10 h](../Documents/fundamental_principles/10_The_General_Social_Work.md#h-the-growth-process-of-communism)).

With these conditions, the idea is an addition to the GIC's model, not something already in it. The GIC may still object that any price that departs from labour time breaks the "law of average production time" ([ch. 10 f](../Documents/fundamental_principles/10_The_General_Social_Work.md#f-the-marxist-solution)).
