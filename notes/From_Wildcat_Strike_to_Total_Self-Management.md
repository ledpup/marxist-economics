# Sources and checks: *From Wildcat Strike to Total Self Management*

Notes on where [From Wildcat Strike to Total Self Management](../Documents/From_Wildcat_Strike_to_Total_Self-Management.md) quotes from, and on the copies used to check the text.

## Copies of the text used for checking

Copies kept in this repository as evidence:

- [De la grève sauvage à l'autogestion généralisée](../Documents/De_la_greve_sauvage_a_l_autogestion_generalisee.md): the French original, from libcom
- [Contributions to the Revolutionary Struggle, chapters 1–2](From_Wildcat_Strike_to_Total_Self-Management/Contributions_to_the_Revolutionary_Struggle-Anarchist_Library.md): the Anarchist Library's copy of Sharkey's translation
- [Counter-Planning on the Shop Floor](From_Wildcat_Strike_to_Total_Self-Management/Counter-Planning_on_the_Shop_Floor.md): Bill Watson's essay, the source of 2.64

Knabb's page for chapter 3 differs from the SI Online copy only in trivial ways ("co-opted", the case of "To" in two headings), so it isn't kept.

The two Sharkey copies sometimes differ in wording, such as "direct" against "reckon" in 2.13, or "unsought" against "unlooked for" in 2.33. Those look like differences between editions, not errors, so the repository copy keeps its own wording. Where only the SI Online copy has an obvious scanning error (for example "he" for "lie" in 1.2d, or "tot" for "lot" in 1.19h), it was corrected from the other copy.

### Readings checked against the French

Some odd readings appear in both English copies, so they are probably in the printed translation. The French shows what was meant:

A place where the translation reversed the meaning of the French, now corrected:

- 2.19: Sharkey has "The voice of the masses is not easily heard above the din of battle." The French, « La voix des masses ne se fait bien entendre que dans le fracas des armes », means "The voice of the masses is only really heard in the clash of arms." The sentence now reads "The voice of the masses is only really heard in the din of battle.", keeping Sharkey's "din of battle". This is an editorial correction of the translation, not of a scanning error.

## Chapter 2, paragraph 64: the Detroit engine plant, 1968

Ratgeb quotes a long account of sabotage "in 1968 in a car plant near Detroit". He cites "the pamphlet *Lordstown 72* published by 4 millions de Jeunes Travailleurs, Paris, See Also *Solidarity Pamphlet 45*".

**The original is Bill Watson, "Counter-Planning on the Shop Floor", first printed in *Radical America*, May–June 1971.** Online at [libcom.org](https://libcom.org/article/counter-planning-shop-floor-bill-watson), with a copy [here](From_Wildcat_Strike_to_Total_Self-Management/Counter-Planning_on_the_Shop_Floor.md). Watson spent a year in "an auto motor plant (Detroit area, 1968)". Every episode in Ratgeb's quotation is in it: the sabotage of the six-cylinder motor and then the V-8s, the inspectors' rejections, the interrogation by the supervisor, the rotating sabotage rota, the rod-blowing contest, the hose fight and the shower cap.

Not yet verified: the French pamphlet *Lordstown 72*, and what *Solidarity Pamphlet 45* contains.

### How the text reached Sharkey

Watson's English became French in *Lordstown 72*, then went into Ratgeb's French book, and Sharkey translated that back into English. So the passage in the book isn't Watson's wording, and details have shifted on the way:

| Watson (1971) | Sharkey's translation of Ratgeb |
| --- | --- |
| "with mixed feelings and motives among those involved — some determined, some revengeful, some just participating for the fun of it" | "with mixed feelings and motives among the workers concerned — certain of them were determined, others were merely out for some sort of revenge, still others took part only for the hell of it" |
| "the systematic cracking of oil-filter pins, rocker-arm covers, or distributor caps with a blow from a timing wrench" | "a simple twist of a monkey-wrench on the oil filter or valve cover or distributor" |
| "As with the six-cylinder motor sabotage, the V-8s were defectively assembled or damaged en route so that they would be rejected." | "As six cylinder jobs the V-8s were assembled in a faulty manner or damaged during production so that they would be rejected." |
| "asserting again and again that their interests were as one with the company[’]s in getting out the best possible product" | "they argued back that their interest and the company's were identical and thus that they had a duty to ensure that only products of the finest quality left their plant" |
| "The inspectors organized a rod-blowing contest … to neglect the torquing of bolts on rods for a random number of motors so that there would be loose rods" | "The inspectors on the engine tests bench decided upon a competition centering upon the valves … a certain number of engines chosen at random would not have their valves soundly attached" |
| "felt the telltale knock in the water-pump wheel" | "heard dubious vibrations" |
| "the rod blowing through the side of the oil pan and across the shop" | "the adjustable valve holding the gearbox would hurtle at great speed from one end of the workshop to the other" |
| "This particular contest went on for several weeks, resulting in more than 150 blown motors." | "This competition continued for several months, and involved the explosion of more than 150 engines." |
| "The fight usually involved about 10 or 15 unused hoses, each with the water pressure of a fire hose." | "All in all some 10 to 15 water jets were engaged in the battle, each one with a pressure force comparable to that of a fireman's jet." |
| "One man walked around with his wife's shower cap on for a few days" | "One fellow strolled around wearing his wife's shower cap" |

Notes:

- The dash after "the workers concerned" was missing from both online copies of Sharkey's text. It was restored by comparison with Watson and the French (« chez les ouvriers concernés - certains déterminés »).
- "Several weeks" became "several months" in the French (« plusieurs mois »), and Sharkey followed it.
- Sharkey's "As six cylinder jobs" is a cramped rendering of the French « Comme les six cylindres » ("Like the six-cylinders"), which in turn shortens Watson's "As with the six-cylinder motor sabotage". It was left as it is, because it is the translation's wording.
- The libcom text has its own scanning errors, such as "company 5" for "company's", and "~ to 50" where Sharkey has "from 1 to 50".
