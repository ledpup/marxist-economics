# Objective

The development of capitalism is leading to increasingly serious crises, reflected in ever-increasing unemployment and an ever-deeper disruption of the production system, leaving millions of workers out of production and starving to death. At the same time, the contradictions between the different nations are increasing, as a result of which the economic war leads to a new world war.

Increasing impoverishment and growing insecurity of existence force the working class to begin the struggle for the communist mode of production. In this struggle, the Group of International Communists encourages the workers to take over the management and administration of production and distribution **themselves** in accordance with generally adopted social rules to realize the **Association of Free and Equal Producers**.

The GIC sees the essential progress of the workers' movement in the development of workers' self-confidence. Therefore, it confronts the leading politics of the parliamentary parties and the trade union movement with the slogan:

**<u>All power to the workers' councils</u>**

**Production in the hands of the enterprise organizations**

GIC
