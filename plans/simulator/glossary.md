# Glossary

The terms the simulation uses on screen and in code, the term in the English translation of the *Fundamental Principles* where it differs, and the Spanish. Definitions are in [The economic model of the *Fundamental Principles*](../../notes-opus5_5/fundamental-principles-economic-model.md).

**The Spanish is provisional.** Check it against a Spanish edition of the *Fundamental Principles* and the usual Spanish translations of Marx's *Critique of the Gotha Programme* before it is fixed. Also decide on Spain's Spanish or a Latin American one ([Internationalisation](internationalisation.md)).

## Changed from the book

The brief replaces three terms ([Plans](README.md#decisions-taken-from-the-brief)):

| Simulation | Book (translation) | Spanish | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Labour certificate** | consumption money | certificado de trabajo (Spanish translations of Marx may use *bono de trabajo*: to check) | Marx's term; avoids "money" |
| **Direct transfer** | giro settlement, giro transaction | transferencia directa | "Giro" is unfamiliar outside Continental Europe ([note](../../notes-opus5_5/fundamental-principles-economic-model.md#old-words-given-new-content)) |
| **By-product method** | (not in the book) | método del subproducto | The method chosen for joint products |

The giro office becomes the **public accounting office**, from the book's own "general social accounting" (contabilidad social general).
