# Workers' democracy

How people organise social work: who runs a workplace, who decides its hours, who may join it or be asked to leave it, and what society owes those who don't work. Most of this sits above the economic structure, the books and the payout factor, but it decides who sets the rules the simulation runs on.

Two models are contrasted: the GIC's *[Fundamental Principles](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/README.md)*, which this project follows, and Cockshott and Cottrell's *[Towards a New Socialism](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/README.md)* (TNS). Pannekoek's *[Workers' Councils](../../Documents/workers_councils/README.md)* fills in what the GIC says only in outline. The positions marked *Patrick's position* are his ([gap 47](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)); the open questions are in [gap 48](gaps-in-the-brief.md#the-economics).

## Summary

- **The workers' council runs the operational unit.** The workforce in general assembly, with delegates where a unit is too big to meet as one, decides how its work is organised. Not a manager, not a union, not a planning authority.
- **Councils, not trade unions.** A union bargains over the price of labour power with an employer. Without wages and employers there is nothing for it to bargain over, and history shows unions turned into the instrument that disempowers councils.
- **The council can dismiss** (*Patrick's position*). This is where we break with TNS, which removes the firing sanction. Dismissal is a last resort, decided by the workforce, and the person is free to work elsewhere.
- **A job for those who want one, not full employment** (*Patrick's position*). Those unable to work are supported regardless. Those who refuse to take part are left to their choice. Nobody is obliged to work.

## Councils, not trade unions

### What each is

| | Trade union | Workers' council |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who belongs** | Members of a trade, by card and dues | Everyone who works in the unit, whatever their trade or party |
| **Organised by** | Trade or industry, across many workplaces | Workplace (the operational unit), joined up through delegates and a congress |
| **What it does** | Bargains with an employer over wages, hours and conditions | Runs the work itself: hours, shifts, who does what, admission, dismissal, the unit's books |
| **Who decides** | Officials prepare, members vote, officials negotiate | The assembly prepares and decides; delegates carry instructions and report back |
| **Presupposes** | Wage labour and an employer on the other side of the table | Common ownership of the means of production, and books open to all |

### Why councils

1. **A union is a seller of labour power.** Its job is to get a better price for it. The GIC treats collective bargaining as the mark of state capitalism: in Russia "the Supreme Economic Council must buy the labor power on the market using the method of the collective agreement with the trade unions, which is also used in Western capitalism" ([2 d](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/02_The_Social_Democratic_Revision_of_Marxism.md#d-distribution-of-means-of-production-and-consumer-goods-in-kind-as-a-bolshevik-ideal10)). Under labour-time accounting there is no wage: one hour worked is one hour credited ([gap 10](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)). There is no employer either: the unit is "a collective legal entity which is responsible to the community for its management" ([14b b](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/14b_The_Control_of_Operational_Life_II.md#b-control-under-communism)). With nothing to sell and nobody to sell it to, a union has no function.
2. **The council includes everyone in the workplace.** In the Russian councils of 1917, "the question was not: 'Which party or union do you belong to?' It was completely indifferent. As an **operational unit**, the decisions were made, the class unit went beyond the fragmented spirit of the membership cards" ([14a d](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/14a_The_Control_of_Operational_Life.md#d-the-destruction-of-workers-control-by-the-bolsheviks)). A union divides a workforce by trade; a shoe factory's cordwainers, designer and cleaners would sit in different unions.
3. **Unions separate officials from members.** "In the unions there was usually a division of task between the officials and the members; the officials prepared and devised the proposals and the members voted" ([Pannekoek, I.3](../../Documents/workers_councils/13_Shop_Organization.md)). The officials become a bureaucracy that asks "not solidarity, the proletarian virtue, but discipline, obedience to the decisions" ([II.1](../../Documents/workers_councils/21_Trade_Unionism.md)). In the council the workers "have to do everything themselves, the conceiving, the devising, as well as the deciding", and its committees "are central, but not ruling bodies, they are no governing board" ([I.3](../../Documents/workers_councils/13_Shop_Organization.md)).
4. **Unions normalise capitalism.** Pannekoek grants their history: they raised workers "out of their helpless misery". But "by the power of the unions capitalism is normalized; a certain norm of exploitation is universally established", and "though products of the workers' fight, kept up by their pains and efforts, trade unions are at the same time organs of capitalist society" ([II.1](../../Documents/workers_councils/21_Trade_Unionism.md)).
5. **History: unions were used to break the councils.** In January 1918 the Bolsheviks merged the factory committees into the unions; all independent factory funds were banned; membership became "an 'official obligation'"; only unions recognised by the central council were legal; and in April 1918 the director's individual responsibility replaced accountability to the workers ([14a d](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/14a_The_Control_of_Operational_Life.md#d-the-destruction-of-workers-control-by-the-bolsheviks)). The GIC adds that German Social Democracy did the same a year later, taking the councils into the union apparatus as legal works councils.
6. **Even a revolutionary union is the wrong carrier.** Anarcho-syndicalism has the unions take over production: "Workers must organize themselves into industrial and agricultural federations so that **their** organizations can take over operational life after the revolution", the CNT demanding "the union's control over production" ([5 e](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/05_Libertarian_Communism.md#e-anarcho-syndicalism)). The GIC objects that this puts an organisation, not the producers, in charge, and makes its size the "yardstick" of whether a revolution is possible.

TNS takes the opposite view of councils: "Soviets are transitory institutions, not lasting forms of state structure" ([13](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/13_On_Democracy.md#direct-democracy-or-soviet-democracy)). Its democracy is demarchy: "the various organs of public authority would be controlled by citizens' committees chosen by lot", partly drawn from the workers concerned ([13](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/13_On_Democracy.md#the-acephalous-state)). The workplace is not its basic unit; the planning authority, the employment agency and the juries are.

## What the council decides

*Patrick's position:* the council decides how its own work is organised. Where a decision draws on the social product, and so on everyone's payout factor, it belongs to the congress of councils. The GIC has the congress set general standards within which units act, such as the 10 % limit on expanding a unit ([13 c](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/13_The_Expansion_of_Production.md#c-the-general-rule-for-the-expansion-of-the-operational-unit)), and treats the length of the working day as a social decision ([13 b](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/13_The_Expansion_of_Production.md#b-the-expansion-of-production-is-always-at-the-expense-of-consumption)).

| The unit's council | The congress of councils |
| --- | --- |
| The length of the working day and week ([gap 44](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)) | The production plan, and each unit's production budget within it |
| Shifts, and which days the unit runs | The payout factor and how it is set ([gap 7](gaps-in-the-brief.md#the-economics)) |
| Full-time and part-time patterns, days off ([gap 9](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)) | Who is paid as public work: child carers, students ([gap 8](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)) |
| Artisan hours: boutique days and the trainee route ([gap 46](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)) | Credits for those who don't work: retirement, illness ([gap 9](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)) |
| Who joins the unit, and dismissal | General standards: expansion limits, the budgets of public units |
| Who does which task, and training | |

The council decides within its production budget: its hours are planned, so a shorter week means more people or less output, and the books show which. The GIC's accounting is what makes a unit's choices visible to everyone else ([14b b](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/14b_The_Control_of_Operational_Life_II.md#b-control-under-communism)).

## Dismissal: where we break with TNS

### TNS removes the firing sanction

TNS describes how capitalism keeps people working: an employment contract "backed up by the ultimate sanction of firing", whose force depends on unemployment. Then: "In a socialist economy the unemployment sanction is quite deliberately removed. Work is guaranteed for all" ([2](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/02_Eliminating_Inequalities.md#differential-payment-for-personal-qualities)). In its place:

- **Grades of pay.** Workers are graded A, B or C by productivity, and paid more or less than one token an hour. The GIC would reject this as a wage differential; so do we ([gap 10](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)).
- **Projects close, people don't.** Planning may close a project; its workers are "assigned elsewhere", and "do not have the right to permanent employment in any particular enterprise or industry (although they do have the right to employment as such)" ([9](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/09_Planning_and_Information.md#rewards-and-sanctions)). They find a new project through a state employment agency ([14](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/14_Property_Relations.md#rights-of-planning-and-economic-projects)).

So TNS does move people out of workplaces, but only through Planning, judging a project's performance, never through colleagues judging one person. The book leaves open whether a project can remove a single worker at all ([TNS note](../../notes-opus5_5/towards-a-new-socialism-economic-model.md#firing-and-redeployment)).

### The council can dismiss

*Patrick's position:* a shop or factory controlled by its workers' council can't function without the ability to dismiss someone. There are cases where it is the only practical option: someone who won't do the work, endangers others, or makes the collective unworkable. Dismissal doesn't stop that person from seeking work elsewhere.

What makes this different from capitalist firing:

- **The workforce decides, not a manager.** The GIC's model is Marx's on the Commune: "the right to appoint and dismiss" placed officials "under the control of the masses" ([14a e](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/14a_The_Control_of_Operational_Life.md)). The same assembly that admits a person can ask them to leave.
- **It is not a sentence to hunger.** Pannekoek wants "the praise or blame of the comrades" to replace "fear for hunger and perpetual risk of losing the job" ([I.3](../../Documents/workers_councils/13_Shop_Organization.md)), and the GIC objects when Leichter has a failed manager brought "to the subsistence level" as punishment ([9 d](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/09_Average_Working_Hour_as_Basis_of_Consumption.md#d-the-consumption-money-by-leichter)). Without unemployment as a system, dismissal from one unit doesn't carry that threat: the person keeps their household's certificates already earned and the free services everyone gets, and is free to join another unit.
- **Not from above.** The GIC rejects closures decided "from above" that cause a workforce "to hit the pavement" ([9 g](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/09_Average_Working_Hour_as_Basis_of_Consumption.md#g-the-distribution-of-the-means-of-production)). No congress or authority dismisses individuals; only a unit's own workforce does.

The weak point is the one TNS's guarantee protects against: a majority that dismisses a minority unfairly. Whether a dismissed worker can appeal, and to whom, is open ([gap 48](gaps-in-the-brief.md#the-economics)).

## A job for those who want one, not full employment

### TNS: a guarantee and an obligation

TNS has two halves. The guarantee: "Work is guaranteed for all", limited to "such work as she is able to perform and for which there is a need" ([14](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/14_Property_Relations.md#individual-property-rights)). The obligation: a flat labour tax that says "each able-bodied person of working age is obliged to perform a basic amount of work for the commonwealth", and a rejection of a guaranteed income because it "effectively allows people to choose subsidised idleness" ([7](../../Documents/towards_a_new_socialism/07_Macroeconomic_Planning_and_Budgetary_Policy.md#tax-policy)).

### Our position

*Patrick's position:* full employment is not a demand a post-capitalist society needs to meet its goals. As a demand that everyone work, it is a totalitarian notion, and it is plainly impossible. Some people are born unable to work; some may simply refuse to take part in social life.

- **Those unable to work** are supported regardless: credited hours through the payout factor, as decided in [gap 9](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided). This is Marx's "funds for those unable to work".
- **Those who refuse** are left to their choice. They get no certificates, since certificates are issued for hours worked or credited, but nobody compels them to work, and they keep what is free to all: child care, school, electricity and water at home. They may soon find such an existence impractical.
- **Those who want work** should find it. "A job for those that want one" is the aim, not a guarantee written into the plan. Units take people on within their planned labour budgets, so whether there is a place depends on the plan, which the councils draw up ([unemployment note](../../notes-opus5_5/unemployment-and-inability-to-work.md#finding-another-place-no-direction-of-labour)).

This is close to the GIC's "those who do not work will not eat" ([16](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/16_The_Economic_Dictatorship_of_the_Proletariat.md)), which was aimed at the propertied classes, but without the compulsion: no certificates, and no direction of labour either. Under direction of labour, the GIC says, "all *individual* freedom has ceased", and it calls the result "enslavement of the working class" ([12 b](../../Documents/fundamental_principles/12_The_Abolition_of_the_Market.md#b-the-supreme-economic-council-distributes-the-labor-power)).

## The two models side by side

| | *Fundamental Principles*, as we read it | *Towards a New Socialism* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who runs a workplace | Its workers' council | A project, under Planning; the workers on it have a say in who joins |
| Democracy above the workplace | A congress of councils | Votes on budgets, and juries chosen by lot |
| Who sets hours and shifts | The unit's council | Each worker's contract with the employment agency |
| Pay | One hour, one hour credited | One token an hour, adjusted by grade A, B or C |
| Employment | A job for those who want one | Guaranteed, and a basic amount obligatory through the flat tax |
| Dismissal of a person | By the council, as a last resort | None; projects close and their workers are reassigned |
| Sanction for low effort | Colleagues' blame; dismissal in the end | Regrading to C, at lower pay |
| Those who refuse to work | Left to their choice, without certificates | Taxed into a basic amount of work |
| Trade unions | No function: no wage, no employer | Not discussed for socialism |

## What the simulation takes from this

- **Each unit has a council, and its rules are the council's.** The working week, shifts, part-time patterns and boutique days are set per unit, with the scenario giving defaults. The scenario's other policies stand for the congress's decisions. → [Data model](data-model.md#operationalunit)
- **Councils are not modelled as meetings in the first builds.** Their decisions are inputs. A decision taken during a run (a shorter week from next month) is a logged command, like any other ([gap 35](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)). Modelling assemblies and votes is for later.
- **Employment can end**: by choice, by retirement, by the unit shrinking, or by dismissal. → [Data model](data-model.md#employment)
- **Dismissal comes with dishonesty** ([gap 45](gaps-in-the-brief.md#decided)): a worker caught cooking the books or shirking can be dismissed by their council, and then seeks a place elsewhere.
- **A person can be outside every unit**, by choice, and is credited nothing. The screen should show them.
