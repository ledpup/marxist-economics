# Karl Kautsky

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Karl Kautsky** (16 October 1854, Prague – 17 October 1938, Amsterdam) was a leading theorist of orthodox Marxism and for decades the chief intellectual voice of the SPD and the Second International. He drafted the theoretical part of the SPD's 1891 Erfurt Programme and edited *Die Neue Zeit*. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Kautsky)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The GIC's main opponent on the working-time calculation. Kautsky describes labour money accurately, then declares it impossible in practice; the GIC answers his objections one by one in chapter 8.
