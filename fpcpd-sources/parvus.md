# Alexander Parvus

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Alexander Parvus** (born Israel Lazarevich Gelfand; 27 August 1867, Berezino – 12 December 1924, Berlin) was a Russian-born Marxist theorist, journalist and activist, prominent in the SPD. He co-developed the theory of permanent revolution with Trotsky and in 1917 helped arrange Lenin's return to Russia. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Parvus)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** “The well-known left-wing Marxist”, quoted to show the left of social democracy also saw socialism as state production.
