# Otto Neurath

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Otto Neurath** (10 December 1882, Vienna – 22 December 1945, Oxford) was an Austrian philosopher of science, sociologist and political economist, a leading figure of the Vienna Circle, an advocate of economic calculation in kind rather than in money, and inventor of the ISOTYPE pictorial statistics. He ran a central planning office in Munich and was imprisoned after the Bavarian Soviet Republic fell. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Neurath)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The leading theorist of the “natural economy” without a unit of account, which the GIC rejects.
