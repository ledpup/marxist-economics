# Dirk Jan Struik

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Dirk Jan Struik** (30 September 1894, Rotterdam – 21 October 2000, Belmont, Massachusetts) was a Dutch-born mathematician and historian of mathematics who settled in the United States in 1934. A committed Marxist, he joined the Communist Party of the Netherlands in 1919 and stayed a member for life. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Jan_Struik)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted at the start of the book to show how new the idea of the workers' councils was in 1919.
