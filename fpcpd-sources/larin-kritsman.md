# Yuri Larin and Lev Kritsman

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Yuri Larin** (17 June 1882, Simferopol – 14 January 1932, Moscow) was a founding member of the Supreme Council of the National Economy (Vesenkha) in December 1917, and in March 1918 drafted the decree on the management of nationalised industry. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Larin)

**Lev Kritsman** (also Kritzman; 1890 – c. 1937) was a Soviet economist and agrarian expert, a Menshevik from 1905 who joined the Bolsheviks in 1918, author of *The Heroic Period of the Great Russian Revolution*, a defence of war communism, and head of the Agrarian Institute of the Communist Academy. He died in the purges. No Wikipedia article; see [Marxists Internet Archive glossary](https://www.marxists.org/glossary/people/k/r.htm) and [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/kritzman-lev-natanovich).

The GIC takes the quote from **Arthur Rosenberg** (19 December 1889, Berlin – 7 February 1943, New York), a Marxist historian and Reichstag member 1924–28, whose *A History of Bolshevism* appeared in 1932. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Rosenberg)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted second-hand on why workers' control under the Bolsheviks was a half-measure.
