# Wilhelm Liebknecht

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Wilhelm Liebknecht** (29 March 1826, Giessen – 7 August 1900, Berlin) was a principal founder of the SPD. He co-founded the Social Democratic Workers' Party with August Bebel in 1869 and was the main architect of the 1875 Gotha congress that merged it with the Lassalleans. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Liebknecht)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** “The old Liebknecht” stands for the early SPD's rejection of state socialism.
