# Arthur Müller-Lehning

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Arthur Lehning** (Paul Arthur Müller-Lehning; 23 October 1899, Utrecht – 1 January 2000, Lys-Saint-Georges) was a Dutch writer and historian associated with anarchist politics. He received the P. C. Hooft Award. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Lehning)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The GIC agrees with his demand that anarchism answer “what to do the day after the revolution”, but argues that his syndicalist programme gives the unions, not the workers' councils, control of production.
