# Alfons Goldschmidt

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Alfons Goldschmidt** (28 November 1879, Gelsenkirchen – January 1940, Mexico City) was a German journalist, economist and university lecturer. He co-founded the League for Proletarian Culture in 1919, co-edited the *Räte-Zeitung*, and arrived in Moscow on 1 May 1920. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfons_Goldschmidt)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** An eyewitness source for early Soviet economic policy: the printing of money and congress resolutions on nationalisation.
