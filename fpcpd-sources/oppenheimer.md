# Franz Oppenheimer

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Franz Oppenheimer** (30 March 1864, Berlin – 30 September 1943, Los Angeles) was a social theorist and economist whose work centred on the sociology of the state. He is best known for *Der Staat* (1907) and is associated with liberal socialism. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Oppenheimer)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** A non-Marxist the GIC agrees with: for Marx, society is “ripe” for socialism only as a whole.
