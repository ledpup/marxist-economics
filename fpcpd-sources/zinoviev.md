# Grigory Zinoviev

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Grigory Zinoviev** (23 September 1883, Yelizavetgrad – 25 August 1936, Moscow) was a Russian revolutionary and Soviet politician, a close associate of Lenin before 1917, chairman of the Communist International from 1919 to 1926, and a Politburo member for much of the 1920s. He was executed after a show trial. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grigory_Zinoviev)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted second-hand (via Pollock) on the Bolshevik hope of abolishing money during war communism.
