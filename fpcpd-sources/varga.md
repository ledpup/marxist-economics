# Eugen Varga

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Eugen Varga** (born Eugen Weiss; 6 November 1879, Budapest – 7 October 1964, Moscow) was a Hungarian communist revolutionary and Soviet economist. He was People's Commissar of Finance in the Hungarian Soviet Republic in 1919, then fled to the Soviet Union and became an economic adviser to Stalin. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugen_Varga)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The GIC's main source on Soviet Hungary, where state communism was put into practice. Quoted on the economy in kind, pricing policy and state control.
