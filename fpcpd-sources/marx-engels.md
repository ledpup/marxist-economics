# Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Karl Marx** (5 May 1818, Trier – 14 March 1883, London) was a German philosopher, social and political theorist, journalist and revolutionary socialist. He developed historical materialism, and his critique of political economy, *Das Kapital*, was his major work. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Marx)

**Friedrich Engels** (28 November 1820, Barmen – 5 August 1895, London) was a German philosopher, social and political theorist and revolutionary socialist. He collaborated with Marx for life, co-wrote *The Communist Manifesto* (1848) with him, and after Marx's death edited and completed the second and third volumes of *Das Kapital*. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Engels)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** The GIC's authority throughout. Marx and Engels are quoted to show that the socially average working hour as unit of account, and labour certificates as the measure of individual consumption, were already their position. Most works are Marx's alone; *Anti-Dühring*, *The Origin of the Family* and the 1891 introduction to *The Civil War in France* are Engels's alone; the *Manifesto* is joint. The **By** column says which.
