# Rudolf Hilferding

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Rudolf Hilferding** (10 August 1877, Vienna – 11 February 1941, Paris) was an Austrian-born Marxist economist and the chief theoretician of the SPD in the Weimar Republic. He was German Finance Minister in 1923 and 1928–29; his major work, *Das Finanzkapital*, appeared in 1910. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Hilferding)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Source of the “General Cartel”, which the GIC treats as the generally accepted picture of communism: a central authority that distributes the product without money or a unit of account.
