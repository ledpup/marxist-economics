# Mikhail Tomsky

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Mikhail Tomsky** (born Mikhail Pavlovich Yefremov; 31 October 1880, Kolpino – 22 August 1936, Bolshevo) was a factory worker, trade unionist and Bolshevik politician who chaired the All-Union Central Council of Trade Unions in the 1920s and sat on the Politburo. Associated with the Right Opposition, he fell in 1928 and took his own life in 1936 to avoid arrest. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Tomsky)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted as the voice of the Soviet unions: wage differences, the refusal to back wage demands, and compulsory union membership.
