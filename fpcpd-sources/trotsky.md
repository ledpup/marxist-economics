# Leon Trotsky

[↑ Sources quoted in the *Fundamental Principles*](README.md)

**Leon Trotsky** (born Lev Davidovich Bronstein; 7 November 1879, Yanovka – 21 August 1940, Mexico City) was a revolutionary and Soviet leader who played a central role in the 1905 Revolution, the October Revolution and the Russian Civil War, and built the Red Army. After falling out with Stalin he was exiled in 1929 and later assassinated in Mexico. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Trotsky)

**In the *Fundamental Principles*:** Quoted on the militarisation of labour in 1920, as proof that a central authority that distributes the social product must also distribute labour power.
